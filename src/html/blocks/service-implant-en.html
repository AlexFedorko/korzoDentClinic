Services

Implantation

A reliable way to replace missing teeth and restore confidence in your smile.

What's included

Placement of dental implants to restore one or several missing teeth, including subsequent implant-supported restorations.

Who it's for

Patients with missing teeth looking for a long-lasting alternative to removable dentures or bridges.

What to expect

The dentist performs diagnostics, evaluates your bone tissue, and creates a personalized implantation plan with an estimated treatment timeline.
