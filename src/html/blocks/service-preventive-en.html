Services

Preventive Care

Regular check-ups and preventive treatments are the foundation of a healthy smile — and help you avoid more complex treatment down the road.

What's included

Preventive check-ups, professional oral hygiene, plaque and tartar removal, fluoride treatment and fissure sealing to protect enamel from decay.

Who it's for

A preventive check-up every 6 months is recommended for adults and children alike, whether or not you have any visible complaints.

What to expect

The dentist examines your teeth and gums, performs a professional cleaning if needed, and gives personalized home-care recommendations along with a preventive care plan.
