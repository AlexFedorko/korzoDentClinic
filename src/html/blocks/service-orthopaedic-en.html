Services

Orthopaedic dentistry

Restoring the shape, function and appearance of your teeth with modern prosthetic solutions.

What's included

Zirconia crowns, IPS e.Max restorations, bridges, removable dentures, and restoration of damaged or missing teeth.

Who it's for

Patients with chipped, worn or missing teeth, or anyone looking to replace old, uncomfortable dentures.

What to expect

The dentist examines your teeth and, if needed, takes X-rays to determine the best restoration option, then agrees the treatment stages and timeline with you.
