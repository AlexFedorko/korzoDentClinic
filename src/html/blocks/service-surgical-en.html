Surgical dentistry

Surgical procedures performed with close attention to your comfort and safety.

What's included

Wisdom teeth extraction, complex tooth extraction, and other oral surgery procedures.

Who it's for

Patients with impacted or problematic wisdom teeth, or cases where non-surgical treatment is no longer possible.

What to expect

Before the procedure, the dentist examines you and, if needed, takes an X-ray, explains the procedure, and provides aftercare recommendations.
