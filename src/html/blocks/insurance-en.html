Insurance

Last updated: August 2026

General information

Korzo Dent accepts patients with dental insurance. Coverage terms depend on your specific insurance provider and the plan you have chosen.

How it works

Let our administrator know about your insurance policy before your visit. We will confirm the coverage terms and the list of services covered with your insurance provider.

What to bring

Please bring your insurance policy (or a digital copy) and a valid ID to your appointment. This helps our administrator quickly verify your coverage.

Costs beyond coverage

If the cost of treatment exceeds your insurance coverage, the patient covers the difference. We always inform you of any additional cost before treatment begins.

Get in touch

Our list of insurance partners and coverage terms may change. To check whether your policy is accepted, contact us at +380 99 004 19 93 or korzodent@gmail.com.
